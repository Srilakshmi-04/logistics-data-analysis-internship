# Logistics Data Analysis Internship

This repository contains the work completed during my Logistics Data Analysis Internship.

## Week 1 – Strategic Planning & Data Exploration

### Objective
The objective of Week 1 was to understand the logistics data, identify key business requirements, explore the available data, and define important performance indicators for logistics analysis.

### Work Completed
- Understood the logistics business requirements
- Identified important logistics performance areas
- Explored the available dataset
- Identified relevant data attributes and KPIs
- Studied factors affecting logistics performance
- Defined the scope for further analysis
- Prepared the Week 1 strategic planning and data exploration report

### Key Areas of Analysis
- Delivery performance
- Transportation and logistics operations
- Order and shipment information
- Cost and efficiency
- Customer and service performance
- Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
